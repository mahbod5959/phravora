Getting started

  1. Choose your English level, profession and career goal.
  2. Select a professional speaking mission.
  3. Grant microphone and Speech Recognition access when you choose to practise aloud.

Microphone or Speech Recognition access

Phravora asks for these permissions only when you begin voice practice. If access was denied, open iPhone Settings, select Phravora, and enable Microphone and Speech Recognition before trying again.

What the current build does

The current app provides structured career-speaking missions and local progress tracking. It does not present sample content as personalised AI feedback, and live AI voice coaching is not connected in this build.

Privacy and data requests

Read the Privacy Policy. For privacy questions or a deletion request, email us from the address you want us to reply to and include “Phravora privacy request” in the subject line. Do not include voice recordings, transcripts, passwords or payment details in your message.
