What Phravora processes

On-device learning data. Your selected English level, profession, career goal, difficult speaking situations, practice target, mission history, speaking-time totals, saved vocabulary and compact learning-pattern metadata are stored locally on your device by the current app build.

Voice practice. Phravora uses the microphone and Apple Speech Recognition only after you choose to start speaking practice. In the current build, Phravora does not upload or retain raw audio or speech transcripts on its own servers. Apple’s speech services may process audio under Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Anonymous service identity and session security. Phravora uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create or restore a pseudonymous service identifier. Firebase App Check helps protect the service from abusive or automated requests.

Server-side session metadata. When a protected practice session is reserved, Phravora’s Firebase service stores the anonymous identifier, mission identifier, requested duration, session status and server timestamps. These records enforce usage limits and prevent duplicate sessions. The current backend does not store raw audio, transcripts, provider credentials or personalised AI feedback.

Why we use this information

  • to provide and secure practice-session functionality;
  • to maintain local learning progress in the app;
  • to prevent abuse, enforce session limits and diagnose reliability issues.

Sharing and tracking

The current build does not sell personal data, use advertising SDKs, or use the information described here for cross-app tracking. Firebase provides the authentication, App Check and protected backend infrastructure described above.

Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your device until you remove the app or its local data. Server-side session metadata is retained only as needed for service security, usage enforcement and operational records. You may ask a privacy question or request deletion by emailing the address above. Because the current service uses a pseudonymous account, we may need limited information to locate the relevant service record without asking for sensitive content.

Your choices

You can decline Microphone or Speech Recognition permission; voice practice will then be unavailable. You can change permission choices in iPhone Settings. You may contact us about access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights that apply to you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Phravora’s features or data practices change. A revised date will appear at the top of this page.
