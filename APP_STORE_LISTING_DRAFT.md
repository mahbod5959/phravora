Phravora

## Subtitle (29 characters)

## Promotional text

Practise the professional English moments that move your career forward.

## Description

Phravora helps non-native English speakers prepare for the professional
conversations that matter: interviews, meetings, project updates, client calls,
presentations and networking.

Choose a career goal, select a structured speaking mission, and practise with a
clear objective, relevant vocabulary and completion criteria. Phravora keeps your
mission history, speaking time and useful practice evidence in one focused place.

Built for career-focused English practice:

- Professional missions for interviews, meetings, client conversations and presentations
- A personal speaking plan based on your level, profession and career goal
- Clear mission objectives and success criteria before you start
- Speaking-time, completed-mission and vocabulary progress
- A private, guest-first experience with settings available inside the app

Phravora does not present sample content as personalised AI feedback. When live
coaching is not connected, the app says so clearly rather than inventing scores or
feedback.
