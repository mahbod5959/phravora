# Phravora future milestones

## Guardrails

Do not weaken Firebase App Check, move provider credentials into iOS, deploy paid realtime work, or add StoreKit without a separately approved milestone. Keep normal learning self-contained in the app.

## Milestone 4 — protected learning backend

Validate App Check against deployed protected endpoints, enforce server-authoritative quota/session handling after production-token verification, and add end-to-end failure/retry coverage. Do not add a realtime-provider credential unless explicitly approved.

## Milestone 5 — real voice learning loop

After privacy, retention, cost, and provider review: implement bounded in-app voice missions, feedback, Mistake Memory updates, repair-mission generation, and safe offline/error recovery. Preserve the structured mission loop; do not turn the product into generic AI chat.

## Milestone 6 — native monetization and account continuity

Implement StoreKit 2 Free/Premium products, verified server entitlement handling, native purchase/restore UX, and anonymous-to-Sign-in-with-Apple account linking. Add account deletion and updated privacy/legal disclosures before release.
