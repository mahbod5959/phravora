Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Phravora is a professional English speaking-practice app. This policy explains how the current version handles information.

Information stored on your device

Phravora stores your onboarding choices, speaking plan, completed-mission history, streak, and saved vocabulary locally on your device. This information is used to personalise your experience and is not used for advertising.

Microphone and speech recognition

Phravora requests Microphone and Speech Recognition permission only when you start a speaking mission. Spoken audio is used live for the practice session. Phravora does not save spoken audio as a recording and does not upload audio recordings to our servers. Apple Speech Recognition may process speech according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy practices.

Service integrity and session operation

To protect the service and operate practice-session limits, the app may use Firebase authentication, app-integrity checks, and server functions. The current version uses an anonymous technical identifier and session-related operational data; it does not provide an email/password account or sell personal data.

Data deletion

You can delete locally stored learning data at any time in Phravora: Settings → Delete my data. Removing the app also removes its local app data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children

Phravora is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page before introducing features that materially change how information is handled, such as cloud recordings, analytics, subscriptions, or account linking.
