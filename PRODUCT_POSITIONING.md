# Product positioning

## Product name

**Phravora** is the customer-facing product and brand. `TalkDaily` remains the internal Xcode project name only. Brand use is subject to the separate, non-legal brand-conflict review; it is not a claim of trademark clearance.

## Value proposition

**Structured English speaking practice for work and career.**

The product helps non-native English speakers practise the professional conversations that affect their career: interviews, meetings, presentations, client calls, workplace conversations, and networking. It is not positioned as a general AI tutor or unlimited AI chat product.

## Primary user

A non-native English speaker who needs to communicate confidently at work or while moving into a new role. The product is global; it is not limited to immigrants in any one country.

## Product loop

Professional goal → speaking mission → real voice interaction → mission outcome → personalised feedback → mistake memory → repair mission → progress over time.

The current build implements the local mission, profile, progress, and mistake-memory foundations. It does **not** claim to provide real-time AI feedback; that capability requires a future reviewed integration.

## Experience principle

Phravora is self-contained for normal learning: open the app, choose a goal, start a mission, speak, receive feedback, see improvement, and continue. Voice practice, feedback, Mistake Memory, repair missions, history, settings, support, and future native subscriptions belong in Phravora—not a browser, external checkout, or companion app. A new screen, setting, or step is justified only when it removes friction or creates clear learning value.

## Three-minute App Review flow

1. Launch a fresh install and complete onboarding: English level, role, career goal, difficult situations, and daily target.
2. Open Today and inspect a mission. It shows a professional objective, roles, success criteria, constraints, vocabulary, and a bounded duration.
3. Start and complete the local demonstration mission. The app records a mission completion and speaking minutes without claiming AI evaluation.
4. Open Progress to see completed professional situations, speaking minutes, and the placeholder state for future recurring-pattern coaching.
5. Open Settings to verify the learning plan and on-device-only local history statement.

This flow demonstrates a structured learning product, rather than an open microphone interface or generic chatbot.
